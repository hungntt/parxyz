Approach

How it works From sensor to care decision.

All three products share one foundation: privacy-preserving learning at the edge, and AI that explains itself and accepts correction.

  1. Sense

    Wearables, ambient sensors and home robots capture physiological and movement signals.

  2. Learn at the edge

    Models run on the device and improve through federated learning, so personal data stays at home.

  3. Explain and contest

    Multi-agent reasoning produces explanations that clinicians, carers and patients can question and correct.

  4. Support care

    Insights reach the right person at the right time, and a person always makes the final call.

Privacy by design

Edge-first processing and federated learning keep what leaves a home to a minimum.

Human in the loop

AI suggests; people decide. Every output can be reviewed, challenged and overridden.

Explainable by default

Built on our team's explainable-AI research, from saliency maps to natural-language rationales.

Evidence before claims

We publish our methods in peer-reviewed venues before we promise outcomes.

Team

Researchers and engineers building care technology.

Parxyz brings together computer science, biomedical engineering and health research. Our members are affiliated with the University of New Brunswick in Canada and Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg (FAU) in Germany.

HN

Truong Thanh Hung Nguyen

Founder & CEO

Hung founded Parxyz to bring trustworthy, human-centred AI out of the lab and into homes and clinics. Hung is a PhD candidate in Computer Science at the University of New Brunswick's Analytics Everywhere Lab, working on explainable and contestable AI, self-evolving multi-agent systems, edge computing and biomedical engineering.

Before Parxyz, Hung earned an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence (minor in Biomedical Engineering) from FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg and a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences, both as a DAAD scholar, and worked as an AI Scientist and Business Unit Director at FPT Software.
