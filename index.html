What we work on

Three places where better technology changes daily care

We focus on care that happens outside the hospital, where privacy, explainability and trust matter most.

Health robotics

Assistive home robots that check in, confirm emergencies and support carers, without streaming private video to the cloud.

  • Multi-stage fall detection with robotic vision confirmation
  • Semi-supervised federated learning across homes
  • Edge inference on consumer hardware

Mental health devices

Wearable ECG and heart-rate variability sensing that turns physiological signals into explainable, contestable insights for mental-health care.

  • Psychiatric screening support from wearable ECG
  • Depression symptom annotation with human feedback
  • Language-model explanations clinicians can question

Aging in place

Systems that help older adults live safely and independently at home, while keeping families and care teams in the loop.

  • Pre-impact fall detection with wearables
  • Shared care planning for care teams and families
  • Gait interpretation for Parkinson's disease

Guardian

A privacy-preserving fall-safety system for the home.

  • Pre-impact fall detection on a wearable
  • Federated learning, raw data stays home
  • Robot confirmation before any alert
How it works
A wearable detects the early signature of a fall before impact. Models improve across homes through semi-supervised federated learning, so raw sensor data stays in the household. Before raising an alarm, a home robot visually confirms what happened, which cuts the false alarms that erode trust.
Who it's for
Older adults living independently, their families, and home-care providers.
Built on
Multi-stage fall detection with robotic vision confirmation (2025) · SF2D, Canadian AI 2025 · Pre-impact fall detection, CASCON 2024
Mental health devicesResearch prototype

Heart2Mind

Wearable ECG insights for mental-health care that clinicians can question.

  • Continuous ECG and HRV between visits
  • Plain-language explanations for every result
  • Clinicians can contest and correct the model
How it works
An ECG wearable captures heart-rate variability. AI models estimate patterns associated with psychiatric conditions, explain each result in plain language, and let clinicians contest or correct it. That feedback improves the system over time.
Who it's for
Psychiatrists, mental-health clinics and researchers who need objective, continuous signals between visits.
Built on
Heart2Mind, ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare 2026 · PAKDD 2025 · Depression symptom annotation, IEEE COINS 2026
Aging in placeResearch prototype

CoPlan

Shared care planning for older adults, families and care teams.

  • Role-based AI agents for each stakeholder
  • Reasoning shown as argument graphs
  • Nothing is adopted without human sign-off
How it works
Role-based AI agents draft care-plan options and lay out their reasoning as argument graphs. The older adult, family members, nurses and physicians can each see why a recommendation was made and challenge it before anything is adopted.
Who it's for
Home-care agencies, long-term care providers and community health teams.
Built on
CoPlan, NGEN-AI 2026 · Contestability for multi-agent care systems (2026) · Care plan coordination, FAIEMA 2026

How it works

One trustworthy AI core, from sensor to care decision

All three products share the same foundation: privacy-preserving learning at the edge, and AI that explains itself and accepts correction.

  1. Sense

    Wearables, ambient sensors and home robots capture physiological and movement signals.

  2. Learn at the edge

    Models run on the device and improve through federated learning, so personal data stays at home.

  3. Explain & contest

    Multi-agent reasoning produces explanations that clinicians, carers and patients can question and correct.

  4. Support care

    Insights reach the right person at the right time, and a person always makes the final call.

Privacy by design

Edge-first processing and federated learning keep what leaves a home to a minimum.

Human in the loop

AI suggests; people decide. Every output can be reviewed, challenged and overridden.

Explainable by default

Built on our team's explainable-AI research, from saliency maps to natural-language rationales.

Evidence before claims

We publish our methods in peer-reviewed venues before we promise outcomes.
